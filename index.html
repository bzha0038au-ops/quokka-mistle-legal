Quokka Mistle Support

Quokka Mistle is a merge game played inside TikTok, made by QUOKKA AI PTY LTD. For privacy, purchase, or technical help, contact support@quokkalabs.io.

Common questions

  • Progress: Your island and items are saved in the TikTok app on your device.
  • Energy: Energy refills over time. You can also watch an optional video for more.
  • Purchases: Purchases are paid with TikTok Beans through TikTok. If a purchase did not arrive, email us with the time of purchase; please do not send payment details.
  • Refunds: TikTok handles Beans billing and refunds through its own support process.
