Quokka Mistle

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

This policy describes how QUOKKA AI PTY LTD handles information in Quokka Mistle, a mini game that runs inside TikTok. It may be updated when the game or its providers change.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, items, energy, coins, and settings are stored by the TikTok app on your device. They stay there until you clear them or remove the game. We do not use this data to identify you.

TikTok sign-in

When the game starts, TikTok signs you in silently. If you make a purchase, our game server exchanges that sign-in for a TikTok user identifier (open_id) specific to this game. We use it only to create your purchase orders and deliver what you bought. We do not receive your TikTok username, password, contacts, or messages.

Purchases

Optional purchases are paid with TikTok Beans. TikTok processes the payment and controls the related data under its own policies. Our server keeps a record of each order (the open_id, the product, the order identifiers, the time, and whether it was paid) so that purchases can be delivered and checked. We never receive your payment card or bank details.

Optional rewarded videos

Watching a video is optional. Videos are provided by TikTok's in-app advertising service, which may process device and ad-interaction information under TikTok's policies. We only receive whether the video finished, so that the reward can be given.

Retention

Purchase records are kept while they are needed to deliver purchases, answer support requests, and meet legal and accounting obligations, and are then deleted. Information handled by TikTok is retained according to TikTok's policies.

Children

Quokka Mistle is suitable for a general audience. Players must meet TikTok's own age requirements. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases.

Your requests

You may ask questions, or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, by emailing support@quokkalabs.io. We may need reasonable information to find and verify your records. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.
